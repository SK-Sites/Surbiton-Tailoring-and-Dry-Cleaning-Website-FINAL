Privacy Policy

Who we are

VIP Surbiton Tailoring & Dry Cleaning, 35 Victoria Road, Surbiton, KT6 4JT, is a trading name of Finefix Ltd (company number 12424328), which is responsible for your personal information. Privacy contact: nagim.ariaa@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Booking details (name, email, phone, services and quantities, notes, and an address only if you ask for collection or delivery): to review your booking, contact you, confirm the price, arrange payment and collection/delivery, and carry out the work. Basis: performing our contract with you or taking steps at your request before a contract.
  • Enquiries by phone, WhatsApp or email: to reply to you.
  • Business records such as invoices and payment records: to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not take payment on this website and never collect card details through it.

Who processes it for us

  • Our website hosting provider (website delivery and server logs).
  • Upstash (a secure record of booking requests).
  • Resend (booking notification emails to us, and your confirmation email).
  • Twilio (a short text alert to the business containing your name, order reference and estimated total only).
  • Google Fonts: your browser requests fonts from Google when you visit, which shares your IP address with Google.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK under appropriate safeguards. We do not sell your data and use no advertising or analytics trackers.

How long we keep it

InformationHow long
Booking requests and customer contact details3 years from the date of the booking, then deleted
Booking emails and text alerts3 years from the date of the booking, then deleted
Invoices and payment records6 years, as required for tax and accounting
Website server logsA short period, under our hosting provider's standard settings

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, or request a copy of it. Contact us using the details above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
