Terms & Conditions

Who we are

VIP Surbiton Tailoring & Dry Cleaning, 35 Victoria Road, Surbiton, KT6 4JT, is a trading name of Finefix Ltd (company number 12424328, registered in England and Wales). We provide tailoring, alterations, repairs, dry cleaning, laundry and specialist garment care.

Booking requests

Submitting a booking on this website is a request, not a payment. Nothing is charged online. Nash will reply within 24 hours to confirm the details, the final price, payment and any collection or delivery. A booking is confirmed once these have been agreed with you.

Prices

Our prices are "from" prices and totals shown online are estimates. The final price depends on the garment and the work needed and is confirmed by Nash. Dresses, bridal and bespoke work, duvets, curtains, rugs, upholstery and other specific requests are priced by quote after your order is confirmed. We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added.

Payment

We accept all major payment methods, including American Express. Payment is arranged directly with Nash. For bridal and bespoke work, a 50% deposit is taken before work begins, with the balance paid on completion.

Turnaround

Many services are typically completed within approximately 24 hours, depending on the garment, service required and workload. If your order will take longer, Nash will send you a message.

Your garments

Please tell us about stains, damage or special care needs. Results depend on the garment, fabric, stain and circumstances, so we cannot guarantee that every mark will be removed.

Damage or loss

VIP takes full responsibility for compensation if an item is damaged or lost while in our care.

Uncollected items

Uncollected items are held until collected, for up to 3 months. After that, they are given to charity.

Cancelling

For bridal and bespoke work, if you cancel once the work has been agreed, the 50% deposit covers the cost of the work already committed. See our Refunds, Complaints & Re-cleaning page.

Your legal rights

Nothing here affects your statutory rights, including under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. See also our Privacy Policy.
