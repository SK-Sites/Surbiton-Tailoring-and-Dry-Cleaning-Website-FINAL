Our story

Craftsmanship, with care.

VIP Surbiton Tailoring & Dry Cleaning is an established local business on Victoria Road, specialising in professional tailoring, alterations, repairs, dry cleaning and specialist fabric care.

Tailoring isn't simply making something smaller or larger. It's understanding the garment, what you need from it, and achieving the right silhouette and finish — combining traditional tailoring expertise with professional garment care.

  • 2010 Nash opens his first tailoring and dry-cleaning shop in Teddington.
  • 2021 VIP Surbiton Tailoring & Dry Cleaning opens on Victoria Road, Surbiton.

From the first fitting to the final finish, we take pride in making sure your clothes look their best.

Meet the founder

Meet Nash

Nash Aria · Founder & Master Tailor

Nash grew up in the trade. Raised in a family tailoring and dry-cleaning business, he began learning from his father at the age of ten, mastering stitching, garment-making, design and pattern cutting from an early age.

In 2010 he opened his first shop in Teddington, and in 2021 he opened VIP Surbiton Tailoring & Dry Cleaning on Victoria Road. Today he specialises in alterations of every kind, bespoke tailoring and design, and expert fabric cutting.

Customers describe Nash as highly skilled, friendly and accommodating — someone who takes on complex alterations, stays calm under pressure and keeps to deadlines, always focused on getting the perfect fit.

FAQs

Good to know.

When are you open, and do you deliver?

We are open 7 days a week, 8am to 6pm, and delivery runs until 8pm every day. Choose collection or delivery when you book and Nash will confirm the arrangements with you.

Can you alter a wedding dress or wedding suit?

Yes. Bridal alterations, wedding dresses, veils and formalwear are part of what we do. Bridal and bespoke work is priced by quote.

Which fabrics and items do you look after?

Everyday clothing and workwear, suits, evening wear, wedding dresses, silk, leather, suede, shoes, uniforms and schoolwear, Asian clothing, duvets, curtains, rugs, upholstery and household textiles.

How long does it usually take?

Many services are typically completed within approximately 24 hours, depending on the garment, service required and workload. If your order will take longer, Nash will send you a message.

Do I pay online?

No. Booking online sends us your request, and Nash replies within 24 hours to confirm the details and final price and to arrange payment and collection/delivery.

How can I pay?

We accept all major payment methods, including American Express. Bridal and bespoke work needs a 50% deposit before work begins, with the balance paid on completion.

How much will it cost?

Our prices are "from" prices. Dresses, duvets, curtains, rugs, upholstery, uniforms, Asian clothing, shoe and suede repairs, bridal and bespoke work are quoted after your order is confirmed. We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added.

Do you do uniforms, schoolwear and Asian clothing?

Yes. These are priced by quote, so send us the details and Nash will confirm.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Tell us and we'll first make every reasonable effort to put it right by re-cleaning or redoing the work. See our refunds and re-cleaning page.
