Refunds, Complaints & Re-cleaning

If something isn't right

Tell us as soon as you can. If you have a genuine problem with the result of a service, our first step is to make every reasonable effort to put it right by re-cleaning the garment or redoing the work. Our priority is to achieve the best possible result for you.

Refunds

Where there is a genuine and reasonable basis for a refund and the issue cannot be appropriately resolved by re-cleaning or redoing the work, we will consider a refund. Each case is looked at individually.

What we can and can't promise

Cleaning results depend on the garment, fabric, stain and circumstances. We take great care, but we cannot guarantee that every stain or mark will be removed.

Cancelling a booking

No payment is taken online, so you can cancel a booking request before work begins by contacting us. For bridal and bespoke work, if you cancel once the work has been agreed, the 50% deposit covers the cost of the work already committed.

Your rights

This does not affect your statutory rights. See also our Terms & Conditions.
